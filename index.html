A claim run in three steps

The same process every time, whether you claim monthly or once a year.

Step 1

Upload bank data

Import your bank transactions for the period. DonorBoost matches incoming payments to donors you already know.

Step 2

Reconcile

Review matches, mark anything ineligible in bulk, and defer items you want to check later. Nothing is claimed until you say so.

Step 3

Output to HMRC

Produce the claim for HMRC, with every donation tied to the claim it was reported on so you never claim twice.
